## 1. About this trial

We are building a music-recording program called SoundCraft-AI. This trial tests whether two things
are useful to a singer: (a) a picture of the pitch in your own recording, and (b) a version of your
recording with its level and tone adjusted, so you can compare the two. It is an early test with a
small number of singers. It is **not** a test of you.

Run by: [name, organisation, contact]. Version of this form: [0.1], dated [ ].

## 2. What you would do (about 60 minutes, plus an optional second recording)

- Share a recording of your own singing that you have permission to share, and tell us the key (Sa) you sang it in.
- Look at the pitch of your recording on screen and try a short exercise on one note.
- Answer three short questions about what the screen shows.
- Listen to your recording twice, in two versions with the loudness matched, and choose which you prefer, or keep the original.
- Afterwards, you may, if you wish, send a second recording within 7 days to try again. You do not have to. We will send one reminder.

Some of what you see may have been prepared by hand by the team before your session. We will tell you which.

## 3. What we will collect

- The recording(s) you share, and versions we make of them.
- Your answers and choices, and notes about what you said and did.
- Your self-described vocal range and whether you use MIDI.
- Your contact details, to arrange the session and the optional follow-up. These are kept separately from the recordings.

We will **not** collect payment details, your location, or any recording from your microphone
other than the files you choose to give us.

## 4. Who sees it and where it is kept

- Only [the named team members] will hear your recording or see your answers.
- Your recording is stored on [named device(s)] and is **not** uploaded to any online service, and is **not** sent to any artificial-intelligence model or service.
- We label your files with a code, not your name. The list that links the code to you is kept in a separate place.
- Our findings will describe the group, not you by name.

**We will not use your recording to train any model.** That is not part of this trial. If we ever
wanted to, we would ask you separately and you could say no without affecting anything here.

## 5. Your choices (tick any that you agree to; none is required)

You can take part in the trial without ticking any of these.

- ☐ **A. Internal findings.** Short excerpts (up to 30 seconds) of my recording, or screenshots of its pitch picture, may be shown to the team in our private findings.
- ☐ **B. Public material.** Short excerpts or screenshots may be used in public material about SoundCraft-AI (for example a web page or demo). I would like to be credited as: [name / "a singer" / not credited].
- ☐ **C. Future trials.** You may contact me about future testing, at the details I have given.

Anything not ticked will not happen.

## 6. Keeping and deleting

- Your recordings and any versions we made will be **deleted by [date, for example 90 days after the trial's decision]**, or sooner if you ask.
- You can ask us at any time to delete your recording and your answers. We will do it within [14] days and confirm in writing.
- After deletion we keep a record that contains no audio and no name: the code, the dates, which version you preferred, and counts. It cannot be used to identify you.
- We also keep this signed form, which has your name on it, for [period, for example 12 months] as proof that you agreed, filed apart from everything else, and then destroy it. Your contact details are deleted with your recordings.
- If you ticked B and we have already published something, we will remove it from our own pages when you ask, but we cannot take back copies that others have saved.

## 7. Taking part and leaving

Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any time, during or after the session, without giving a
reason and without any penalty. If you stop, tell us whether to delete what we have or to keep it
for the uses you ticked.

You confirm that you are 18 or older, and that the recording you share is your own performance and
that you have permission to share it and anything in it (for example a song written by someone else).

## 8. Risks and benefits

- We expect no risk beyond everyday listening. You may find the pitch picture of your own voice surprising or uncomfortable. It is a measurement, not a judgement, and you may stop.
- The processed version may sound worse than the original. Choosing the original is a perfectly good result.
- There is no guarantee of benefit. [You will receive: payment/voucher/nothing.]
